## Question and method

Establish a small correctness reference for I01/I02/R03 before choosing complex
estimators. Use exact rational arithmetic, two scalar variables and independent
Gaussian factors. No random seeds, process communication, nonlinear solver or
physical/controller assumptions enter this cycle. Units are arbitrary scalar units;
these are information examples, not calibrated millimetre safety claims.

The full information matrix is solved explicitly. Freezing separately uses a Schur
complement. Closed-form conditional calculations check the answer independently.
Permission variant is P3: the selected measurement may not inform future inference;
the independent priors remain authorized. Positive results must equal the oracle
exactly, with no tolerance. Deliberately incorrect controls must differ.

The toy summary store tests a stable key with increasing revisions, idempotent
duplicate delivery and retained deletion tombstones. It does not implement grants,
concurrent writers, durable storage or a distributed acceptance protocol.

## Findings

1. With independent unit-variance zero-mean priors on x and y, and the relative
   observation x-y=10 with variance 1, the joint mean is (10/3,-10/3), each marginal
   variance is 2/3, and cross-covariance is 1/3. Exact elimination preserves x's current
   marginal. The alternative conditional derivation integrates y into measurement
   variance 2, giving the same result.
2. Dropping cross-covariance gives relative variance 4/3 instead of 2/3, and sum
   variance 4/3 instead of 2. The shortcut can be too pessimistic or overconfident.
3. Ten deliveries of one unit-variance estimate retain variance 1 under replacement.
   Treating them as independent gives variance 1/10. A delayed earlier revision does
   not resurrect a summary after its tombstone, and revision collisions are rejected.
4. Removing the observation from the retained factors and rebuilding restores x's
   authorized prior N(0,1). Keeping the frozen prior retains mean 10/3 and variance
   2/3. Subtracting the raw factor's x-block AFTER elimination gives the wrong mean
   -10 and variance 2. That is a counterexample to this naive subtraction method,
   not a claim that every richer incremental removal representation is impossible.
5. Stronger insufficiency witness: two models use the same observation x-y=0 with
   variance 1. Model A has prior variances (1,1); model B has (4/3,1/3). Both freeze to
   exactly x~N(0,2/3). Withdrawing that SAME observation leaves variances 1 and 4/3,
   respectively. Thus the frozen scalar marginal plus the removed factor alone
   cannot determine the correct future result for both models. Extra retained
   decomposition, authorized reconstruction or an explicit unsupported outcome is
   necessary for this withdrawal class.
6. The harness refuses a finite absolute estimate for a relative-only unanchored
   system. It does not yet recover relative-only queries from a singular graph.

## Implications and limits

This supports starting with a retained authorized factor ledger and replaceable
summaries as the reference. It does NOT show that full replay is fast enough, choose
a production checkpoint representation, or establish nonlinear/SE(3) correctness.
Shared lineage, actual grants, general nesting and distributed tombstones are untested.
The 9 passing tests do not complete the I01, I02 or R03 scenario families.

T01 now has an exact finite insufficiency witness; it was an anticipated design
constraint, not a newly discovered contradiction in the revised provider direction.
T02 remains untested. No other design tension is established by this cycle.
