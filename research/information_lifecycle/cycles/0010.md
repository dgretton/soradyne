## Findings

A frozen first-order likelihood can produce a large correction on rebuilding even
when no new measurement has arrived. Its covariance can remain small and positive
throughout. In the noiseless centered fixture, freezing 90 degrees from the optimum
returns an angle 32.704 degrees wrong. Rebuilding the same four measurements moves
a query point two metres from the body origin by 1.126 m. This is an approximation
failure deliberately induced by a poor linearization, not evidence of real motion.

The simpler successful representation here is a compact **nonlinear** likelihood.
Centered point statistics preserve the entire likelihood for this restricted model,
and reproduce the independently implemented raw batch optimum and local covariance
in all 128 configurations. No iterative solver, damping or smoothing is needed.
This does not establish sufficiency for uncertain landmarks, calibration, perspective
projection, correlated measurements, nested graphs or selective withdrawal.

Preserving the likelihood does not make its local Gaussian covariance globally
accurate. For the weak-baseline, sigma=.2 m, noiseless sample, the full nonlinear
posterior puts only **86.58%** of its angular probability inside the interval formed
as mode +/-1.96 local standard deviations. Both the raw solver and the exact compact
summary have this issue. This is a conditional posterior-mass calculation under the
declared priors, not a frequentist coverage estimate or a Monte Carlo result.

Two additional witnesses establish insufficiency of the **specified frozen quadratic**
plus its identity-only metadata. Different raw likelihoods yield identical anchor,
H, eta and constant yet require different angular uncertainty; a second pair requires
opposite orientations. Merely shifting the quadratic's coordinates cannot recover
the difference. This does not prove that every pose/covariance encoding is insufficient:
a richer model-aware encoding or extra retained statistics changes the premises.

## Model, representation and reference

One T_world_body in SE(2), four exact known body landmarks, identified correspondences,
world measurements z_i=R(theta)p_i+t+e_i, independent isotropic Gaussian noise with
known sigma. The world datum and scale are known; no latent variable is eliminated.
All inputs, raw replay and summaries retain P0 permissions. No permission transition
occurs in this slice. Grants, complete input manifests and local enforcement are
assumptions; zero forbidden IDs is a bookkeeping check, not a revocation-enforcement test.

Internal chart is [theta,t_x,t_y], in radians/metres, float64. This additive chart is
not an SE(2) logarithm. Reported left/world tangent covariance uses
v=delta_t-J*t*delta_theta and retains all cross terms. Point-query covariance agrees
through both charts, with a negative control showing that dropping cross terms changes
the result. The current Nestbox conventions, glossary, SUBSTRATE and E3.5/E3.10/E7.1
were consulted at `041f1146df2e2a271cd0489fda520bc65f2e9f70`. This isolated routine is
not an implementation of the production geometry API or GTSAM's retract.

For centered points p'_i,z'_i, retain n, mean_p, mean_z, S_p=sum||p'||^2,
S_z=sum||z'||^2, a=sum p' dot z', b=sum cross(p',z'), and sigma. Direct expansion gives

    SSE(theta,t) = S_p + S_z - 2(a*cos(theta)+b*sin(theta))
                   + n*||t+R(theta)*mean_p-mean_z||^2.

Thus the summary preserves the complete parameter-dependent likelihood, not just its
value and derivatives at one point. Its optimum is theta=atan2(b,a),
t=mean_z-R(theta)*mean_p when the angular profile is observable. Its centered angular
curvature is hypot(a,b)/sigma^2; centroid translation curvature is n/sigma^2. Transform
the resulting local covariance back to the pose chart, preserving cross-covariance.

The independent raw reference uses centered point clouds and a determinant-corrected
SVD for the proper rotation, then translation. The reference implementation follows
the [NIST algebraic justification of Kabsch–Umeyama](https://math.nist.gov/~JBernal/kujustf.pdf)
and [NumPy's SVD API](https://numpy.org/doc/1.26/reference/generated/numpy.linalg.svd.html).
Raw residual derivatives supply the observed Hessian; finite differences check it.
An 8192-angle raw profile grid checks that it does not beat the SVD optimum. A reflected
point-cloud regression distinguishes a proper rotation from a spurious perfect reflection.

The frozen candidate stores r0+J0*delta at its chosen anchor, reduced to H=J0'J0/sigma^2,
eta=-J0'r0/sigma^2 and a constant. It solves this quadratic once, then interprets its
additive pose as a rigid transform. The recentered control shifts that same quadratic
to its solution and solves again. It does not evaluate the original residual anew.

The exact observed Hessian and the Gauss–Newton J'J are intentionally distinguished.
The former includes residual-dependent second derivatives; the latter is the frozen
candidate's declared approximation. Neither is claimed to be an exact posterior
covariance in a broad nonlinear distribution, nor is this a claim that GTSAM's usual
covariance convention is a coding defect.

## Sweep and numerical checks

Base p=(-1,-.5),(-1,.5),(1,-.5),(1,.5). Scale 1 or .1; body-coordinate centroid (0,0)
or (3,-2). Truth theta=0,t=(.3,-.2). Sigma .05 or .2 m. Noise is either zero or sigma
times the fixed residual pattern in the protocol. Anchors are theta=-delta,t=(0,0),
delta=0,5,15,30,60,90,120,170 degrees. The displaced anchor is prescribed, not the
outcome of an already-converged survey. Noisy optimum displacement is also recorded.

Four geometries x two sigmas x two samples x eight anchors = 128 configurations,
512 method outputs and 1,536 synthetic event records. There are only 16 distinct raw
datasets; repeating an anchor is not independent statistical evidence. No random seeds
or Monte Carlo trials are used. Truth is used only for generation and error evaluation.

The compact summary's 3,200 objective evaluations agree with raw residual evaluation.
Maximum relative objective discrepancy is 3.92e-13; maximum local-covariance relative
error is 3.09e-13; maximum translation discrepancy is 2.59e-15 m. The wrapped angular
discrepancy rounds to zero in float64. Raw Hessian finite-difference relative error
is at most 1.99e-11. The grid beats the SVD by at most 4.17e-17 SSE, numerical noise.
All predeclared equivalence thresholds pass; they are numerical checks, not task limits.

Illustrative diagnostics are <=1 degree angle difference from the raw batch result,
<=1 cm maximum discrepancy at body query points (0,0),(2,0),(0,2), and [.94,.96]
posterior mass for the claimed nominal 95% angular interval. These are declared
diagnostics, not acceptance criteria for hardware. Failure counts overlap.

| Method | Outputs | Angle diagnostic failures | Point diagnostic failures | Interval-mass failures |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Full raw batch | 128 | 0 | 0 | 32 |
| Nonlinear moments | 128 | 0 | 0 | 32 |
| Frozen linear | 128 | 88 | 104 | 84 |
| Recenter same quadratic | 128 | 88 | 104 | 84 |

The 32 raw/moment mass failures are four weak raw datasets repeated at eight anchors.
They must not be described as 32 independent statistical failures. No Gaussian or
uncertainty gate has been fitted to these results.
The angle/point columns measure disagreement with the raw reference. The weak noisy
sample's raw estimate itself differs from truth by 30.70 degrees; its truth-relative
local quadratic error is recorded as well. Solving the likelihood exactly cannot remove
measurement uncertainty.

For angular uncertainty, a uniform angular prior and flat translation prior give a
proper angular marginal proportional to exp(-profile_SSE/(2*sigma^2)); integrating
translation contributes a theta-independent factor. Bounded raw-residual quadrature
uses [SciPy quad](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.integrate.quad.html),
split at the mode, with limit 100 and 1e-10 absolute/relative tolerance. A separate
8192-point periodic sum agrees on normalization to relative 4.39e-16. The largest
normalization error estimate is 3.25e-11. These checks do not replace empirical coverage.

## Jumps, lever arms and missing nonlinear information

Noiseless centered geometry gives the analytic frozen result theta=-delta+sin(delta).
All 64 noiseless configurations match this formula within 1e-10 radians. For scale 1,
sigma=.05 m, selected results are:

| Anchor displacement | Angle error | Centered maximum query error | Offset maximum query error |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 degrees | .00634 degrees | .000221 m | .01372 m |
| 15 degrees | .17076 degrees | .00596 m | .12286 m |
| 30 degrees | 1.35211 degrees | .04720 m | .48305 m |
| 90 degrees | 32.70422 degrees | 1.12616 m | 4.08513 m |

An angular threshold alone therefore does not bound query error for this chart and
geometry. The offset case changes the physical query-point locations relative to the
observed landmarks: it is not a pure coordinate relabeling of the same task. These
numbers do not prove a universal translation-invariance failure or a universal gate.

At tick0 publish from the retained representation, tick1 recenter/repeat with no new
data, tick2 rebuild from the authorized original raw data. Original acquisition count
remains four. Frozen/recentered methods retain their wrong result until rebuilding;
the largest query correction in the grid is approximately 10 m in the deliberately
extreme fixtures. The compact summary's maximum correction is 2.64e-15 m. Total
variation and covariance changes are recorded separately from authorization and
observation count. No velocities, acceleration limits, controller, queue or physical
clearance are modeled; synthetic ticks are not latency measurements.

The two-world witness was declared as a follow-up after inspecting the original grid,
before executing that extension. With centered p, S_p=5, sigma=.2, anchor zero, compare
z=(1+c)p to z=(1-c)p. Both have zero gradient at the anchor, the same frozen
H=diag(125,100,100), eta=0, and constant=125*c^2/2, plus identical IDs and policy.
For c=.5, both full optima are also zero, but angular inverse-Hessian variances are
.005333... and .016, versus the identical frozen .008. Thus even freezing at the
correct optimum can discard relevant likelihood shape in this residual-bearing case.
For c=1.5, full optima differ by pi while the frozen state is still identical.
The algebra establishes insufficiency for this representation and metadata; the tests
check the arithmetic. The data are permitted Gaussian samples, not asserted typical
measurements, hidden changes to a scale parameter, or evidence of a physical bump.

## Design implications and next slice

For Nestbox, distinguish an authorized retained artifact, a faithful likelihood, an
accurate local approximation and an adequate consumer query. None implies the next.
E3.5 needs approximation/uncertainty checks over its intended use range, with model,
linearization and input identities attached. An origin/angle distance alone need not
bound a consumer point's error. A local pose covariance does not establish Gaussian
coverage in weak geometry. Preserve relinearizable state or lawful replay where a
local approximation cannot carry the intended contract; test invalidation and recovery
before relying on smooth consumer behavior. These are proposed tests, not schema edits.

Do not infer that an operational body rotation alone invalidates an intra-body survey:
our fixture freezes a pose-observation likelihood in an additive chart, whereas a
well-designed body-relative structural representation is a different object. A converged
survey, later independent constraints and genuinely eliminated uncertain landmarks must
be tested explicitly. This cycle does not measure their validity range.

For Soradyne, existing opaque representation/revision and retained-state contracts fit
this result. The application determines whether a retained derivative is adequate;
the provider preserves and routes its declared identity and rights. No new numerical
responsibility or protocol change follows. T01/T02 gain evidence; no new architectural
contradiction or physical-safety claim is established.

Next bounded chunk 0011: fixed-seed Monte Carlo uncertainty calibration in this same
known-landmark SE(2) model, with strong/weak geometry and independently drawn Gaussian
observations. Predeclare trial counts, seeds, held-out checks and confidence intervals;
compare local observed-Hessian/Gauss–Newton intervals with intervals obtained from the
full angular likelihood. Keep anchors fixed before sampling and truth out of candidate
solves. This separates deterministic posterior mass from actual repeated-sample coverage.
Then test freezing at a converged survey followed by new independent constraints and
uncertain landmark/calibration elimination; the compact moment formula cannot be
assumed sufficient there. Broader SE(3), timing faults and physical consumers stay queued.
