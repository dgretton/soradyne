## Findings for the contracts

**A complete result and its acceptance must have one durable publication identity.**
Numerical state, uncertainty, exact input coverage, dependency/calibration revision,
model/representation, coordinate and physical-state epochs, applicability, and build
policy are bound by an immutable manifest. A publication record atomically selects it
and records the policy/epoch decision checked at acceptance and an idempotent operation
receipt. The current-use decision is separate again. A correct manifest needs correct
producer assembly: hashing wrong numerical state together with new coverage does not
make that combination mathematically valid.

**Invalidation must survive independently of its replacement.** A grant withdrawal or
epoch close can become durable before a rebuilt answer exists. Recovery then reports
unavailable for the affected current-use query; it cannot silently reinstate the old
result. An unaffected older result may remain usable under its stated coverage. This
does not require a transaction spanning all domains or the network, or synchronously
rewriting/deleting every cached artifact. It does require readers and publishers to
check the applicable durable control state.

**Acknowledgments need named meanings.** Receiving bytes, retaining a complete artifact,
applying a control and publishing a selected result are different promises. In this
candidate, acknowledge publication only after its manifest dependencies and acceptance
record are durable; acknowledge control only after its gate is durable. An operation
may have committed even when its acknowledgment was lost. Retry uses the same operation
identity/content and cannot duplicate evidence or move the head backward. The receipt
confirms a historical commit; it is not a fresh permission to use that result.

These refine existing identity, recovery and validity obligations. **No genuine new
architectural contradiction was established.** The live-serving defect described below
was a model/test error, not a reason to weaken the contract or expand the investigation.

## Assumed primitive and tested scope

One serialized local writer uses an abstract **atomic durable record replacement**.
A completed replacement survives restart; interruption at that atomic step produces
either its whole old or whole new state. Payloads/dependencies/manifests are immutable
durable objects, while staging writes are volatile. A single durable head record binds
the selected manifest, acceptance gate and operation receipt. A crash discards pending
writes; retained caller acknowledgments remain observable outside the crashed process.
The model checks both sides of every atomic step by crashing at adjacent cuts.

This is an assumed storage primitive implemented as dictionary assignment in the
experiment. There are **no filesystem writes implementing that primitive**, fsync tests,
database transactions, torn-sector faults, process concurrency or consensus. Production
must supply an appropriate primitive and validate its implementation. This is a simple
sufficient construction, not a requirement for this exact object layout or database.
An alternative may satisfy the same recoverable-result obligation by another explicitly
guaranteed representation/reconstruction path.

The control rule is deliberately local: after receiving a restrictive event, serving
is blocked until the gate is durable; the update becomes locally effective at that
durable commit and can then be acknowledged. If a crash occurs **before** that commit,
the event is unacknowledged, its volatile knowledge is lost, and the caller must retry.
The restart oracle permits the previous local state in that case. If the application
requires effect from an earlier external time, it needs durable knowledge or fail-closed
authority refresh before serving after restart. That stronger freshness obligation is
not proved here, and cannot be supplied merely by a build-time policy ID. R07/F05 remain
deferred. This distinction must be explicit in the eventual acknowledgment contract.

For the epoch fixture, a newly learned physical-state close has an effective boundary
at time 10; the checked current-use query is at time 15. Before installing the close,
the local state still describes E0 over [0,20). After installation, E0 is closed at 10
and E1 covers [10,20). The event changes knowledge/applicability, not a simulation clock.
Coordinate chart stays the same; this does not reinterpret a physical change as a
coordinate reset. Old historical results are retained, but historical query handling
is not implemented by this current-use fixture. Scope is one result slot, not a global
epoch or counter imposed on independently evolving domains.

## Exact answers and finite crash traces

Independent scalar observations a=0, b=10, c=20 m have variance 1 m². A checkpoint
contains precision and weighted sum plus its exact coverage. Publication does not by
itself make an export a usable checkpoint; the type must declare sufficient restart
state. Replay [b,c,b,c] after the a+b checkpoint must yield unique coverage {a,b,c},
mean **10**, variance **1/3**, matching independent raw averaging.

The allowed-state oracle is a literal table driven by completed events, not by decoding
the candidate's manifest or using its acceptance routine:

| Operation | Old mean / variance | After durable control, before replacement | Published mean / variance | Crash cuts |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Export a -> a+b | 0 / 1 | No control; old remains valid | 5 / 1/2 | 10 |
| Checkpoint a -> a+b | 0 / 1 | No control; old remains valid | 5 / 1/2 | 10 |
| Close E0, publish c in E1 | 5 / 1/2 | Current-use unavailable | 20 / 1 | 13 |
| Strict P3 withdrawal of b | 5 / 1/2 | Current-use unavailable | 0 / 1 | 13 |

For each operation, stage/persist payload, stage/persist dependency, stage/persist
manifest, stage head, atomically publish, then acknowledge. Epoch/grant cases prepend
stage gate, persist gate and acknowledge control. Include the initial cut. At every
cut check live and restarted availability, exact mean/variance, coverage and epoch;
check acknowledged obligations; retry to completion and repeat the same operation once
more. Durable state and result identity must remain unchanged by that second retry.

The corrected candidate passes **46 cuts**, with **18 unavailable restarted states**
and **20 unavailable live states**. The extra two live gaps occur immediately after
receiving a restrictive control but before making it durable. All retries reach the
required new answer with one receipt and no duplicate numerical contribution. Separate
tests cover delayed old publication retry after a later local publication, conflicting
reuse of an operation ID, missing dependencies, a policy change between staging and
publication, and distinct build/acceptance/current-use policy revisions. Local receipt
retention is bounded to these operations; no unbounded deduplication claim follows.

No smoothing or physical trajectory is modeled. The dynamic observations are the
explicit validity gaps, changed means/variances/coverage, and unchanged answers after
duplicate retry. Their adequacy for a moving consumer is not established.

## Negative evidence and the preserved correction

The first run checked recovery correctly but used its restart oracle for live reads
too. It allowed the old result after `stage_gate`, contrary to the protocol's serving
barrier. Review found this after the original evidence had been saved. Both original
code and artifact remain unchanged. The separate corrected candidate blocks that live
read, and the added test preserves the original response as a negative control.

The final artifact contains **17 negative controls** (16 from the original plus the
serving gap):

- Four wrong assemblies pair new coverage with old numerical state. Checkpoint replay
  then gives the **right mean 10 but wrong variance 1/2 rather than 1/3**, despite
  claiming coverage {a,b,c}. Identity/integrity checks alone do not detect a producer's
  consistently mislabeled payload. Correct assembly is a required producer obligation.
- Four premature publication acknowledgments lose their promised revision on crash.
  Four forced heads published without their numerical bytes recover unavailable: a
  reader can reject the broken artifact, but that does not fulfill the durable-publication
  acknowledgment. Fail-closed validation and reliable acknowledgment are separate checks.
- Two acknowledgments of volatile-only controls restore forbidden/closed old current
  results after restart. Two readers trusting build-time policy/epoch alone expose an
  old result even after the restrictive gate is durable.
- The original pending-control live read violates the receive-to-durability barrier;
  the corrected reader returns unavailable without claiming the event is already durable.

These are deliberate counterexamples except for the discovered original serving gap.
No candidate numerical threshold or policy requirement was relaxed to make checks pass.
The original 255-test result did not establish the missing live invariant; the final
**259 tests** include its explicit regression. Ruff initially reported two import-order
errors in the new correction files; they were fixed before corrected artifact generation.

## Minimal obligations for synthesis

1. Bind a semantically coherent numerical artifact and all reconstruction/compatibility
   metadata to an immutable revision. Keep sufficient numerical state separate from
   identity-only coverage. Checkpoint coverage must match the state actually included.
2. State the local atomic acceptance boundary and its durable prerequisites. Record
   acceptance authority alongside the selected revision; current serving may require a
   different authority decision. Preserve invalidation/control state across data recovery.
3. Keep control-effect/acknowledgment semantics distinct from data completeness and new
   result publication. A pending restrictive update needs a serving barrier; a durable
   applied update must not roll back because replacement computation was interrupted.
4. Name operation identities, content binding, retry semantics and the receipt retention
   window. A retry acknowledgment is historical; a duplicate cannot append evidence or
   republish a superseded answer as current. Invalidations must remain recoverable for
   as long as surviving dependent artifacts could otherwise be admitted again.
5. Preserve separate historical/as-of and current-use query semantics. Retention does
   not imply present permission or current applicability. Incomplete/forbidden/closed
   results must not become smooth or apparently precise fallback answers.

Possible owning sections for final proposals: Nestbox's result/checkpoint and epoch
contracts and `SUBSTRATE.md` recovery/custody/publication promises; shared-flow §§2,3,5
for generic acceptance, durable acknowledgments, opaque manifests and recovery. The
provider does not validate application calibration, covariance or checkpoint sufficiency.
No owning document is edited here.
