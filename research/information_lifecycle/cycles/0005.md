## Main findings

Evidence identity and statistical dependence require different accounting. Deduplicating
observation IDs prevents reuse of the same sample, but distinct observations can still
share uncertain calibration. Keeping that shared latent variable during composition,
with its prior included once, works in all 120 tested event/configuration states when
individual contributions remain available. Rebuilding from authorized raw observations
also works whenever the stipulated archive is available.

Two losses of information are established by exact witnesses:

1. Scalar means, variances and disjoint observation IDs do not determine the joint
   dependence. Identical child marginals can require different fused uncertainty.
2. Lineage plus aggregate joint factors does not necessarily recover the numerical
   contribution of an overlap. Two different datasets produce identical retained
   aggregates but require different full-data answers. Retaining a calibration variable
   alone does not fix this separate loss.

Known cross-covariance can still support an honest answer from reduced information.
That answer need not equal the answer from the original data. This distinction matters:
information loss and incorrect uncertainty are different failures, and the consumer
must know which information set an answer represents.

The simplest supported direction remains batch composition/rebuild with explicit
identities and dependency structure. Use disjoint conditional aggregates where the
supported withdrawal/replacement granularity permits them. Preserve finer numerical
components or an authorized replay path where it does not. Report unsupported/full-
coverage unavailable when neither exists. This is a bounded research result, not a
recommendation to store every observation forever or an implemented storage policy.

No new architectural contradiction was established. The overlap witness strengthens
T01; shared calibration was already an explicit I03 investigation target.

## Model, resources and independent reference

The dimensionless unknown is x, with a flat prior. A shared calibration b has the
single prior N(mu,tau2), and each unique observation is z_i=x+b+e_i, with independent
conditional noise variance sigma2. The three fixed observations r1,r2,r3 are 0,2,8.
The prior is independent of their noise and is not itself derived from their outputs.
That independence is an assumption; circular prior construction belongs to I04.

The 24 configurations combine sigma2 in {1,4}, tau2 in {1/4,1,4,16}, and:

- singleton children {r1}, {r2}, {r3};
- unequal disjoint children {r1,r2}, {r3};
- a diamond {r1,r2}, {r2,r3}, where r2 is the very same observation.

The reference changes variables to s=x+b. With n unique authorized observations,
s has mean average(z), variance sigma2/n, and is independent of b. Consequently:

```
mean(x,b) = (average(z)-mu, mu)
Cov(x,b)  = [[tau2+sigma2/n, -tau2], [-tau2, tau2]]
Var(x+b)  = sigma2/n
```

The candidate instead assembles two-variable information matrices/vectors and inverts
them using cycle 0001's exact baseline. Full joint mean, covariance and query variance
are checked, not just the displayed x mean. The reference does not call that assembly.
No calibration prior means no finite absolute x answer; a general relative-query or
null-space API is not implemented in this slice.

A leaf conditional likelihood on (x,b) stores five rational coefficients. An atomic
child preserves these per-observation blocks. A compressed child sums them into one
conditional block plus its source set. A scalar child integrates b out under its
construction-time prior, retaining mean, variance, lineage and prior-version stamp.
The two joint representations contain no calibration prior. The parent supplies the
current one once; the repeated-prior negative control deliberately violates this.

The boundary/model identity denotes the same x and b throughout. A changed prior
belief is allowed without rebuilding the conditional likelihoods. A changed variable,
gauge or model identity is rejected, rather than interpreting old coefficients under
new meanings. This does not demonstrate arbitrary calibration replacement or drift.

Input resources differ deliberately. In state 3 the parent raw archive is absent,
but specifically authorized per-observation conditional blocks may survive. The raw
candidate is a stateless replay comparison, with no hidden cache from earlier states.
The atomic method therefore has more surviving information in that state; its extra
availability is not a free improvement over raw retention. These particular atomic
blocks reveal raw-equivalent values. They are neither a privacy boundary nor a claim
of useful numerical compression. Permissions to retain/use them are fixture inputs.

The recorded payload counts include all blocks in the latest child packets, before
cross-child deduplication or inference filtering. Initial diamond storage is:

| Representation | Numerical coefficients | Lineage entries | Blocks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Atomic joint | 20 | 4 | 4 |
| Compressed joint | 10 | 4 | 2 |
| Scalar marginals | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| Raw archive | 3 values | 3 | 3 |

These are structural counts, not bytes or performance measurements. Fixed model/noise,
prior and version metadata, Python object overhead and transient delivery history are
excluded. A compressed block keeps five numerical coefficients but its lineage grows
with source count. Atomic retention grows with source count too. General separator
size, retained-byte budgets, long histories and rebuild deadlines remain untested.

## Event results and negative controls

Every configuration has five local event states:

0. Initial child revision 1, prior mean 0 and version 1.
1. Equivalent revision 2, duplicated deliveries and late revision 1; no new evidence.
2. Prior replaced by mean 2, variance tau2/4, version 2; still no new observations.
3. Immediate P3 withdrawal of r2, requiring exactly r1/r3; archive unavailable.
4. Authorized children rebuild revision 3 from r1/r3; old and duplicate packets replay.
   An empty child publishes a newer empty packet, preventing old data resurrection.

Current policy is trusted and installed synchronously: effective, learned and enforced
states coincide. This is an inference-use check, not physical erasure or a full rights
ledger. The complete snapshot resolves latest revisions deterministically, including
conflict rejection; the test is not an online delivery-prefix or network-latency proof.
Only current packets contribute to the answer. Source identity is independent of child
packet identity. Reversing the entire delivery order yields identical results.

| Method | Available | Unavailable | Incorrect numerical answers | Forbidden-use outcomes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Atomic joint, one prior | 120 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Compressed joint, supported overlap only | 80 | 40 | 0 | 0 |
| Authorized raw rebuild | 96 | 24 | 0 | 0 |
| Scalar marginals with ID/version checks | 56 | 64 | 56 | 0 |
| Atomic deduplication, prior repeated per child | 120 | 0 | 120 | 0 |
| Joint composition ignoring withdrawal | 120 | 0 | 24 | 24 |

The denominator is 120 states per method, 720 recorded outcomes total. These are fixed
counterexamples/checks, not independent statistical trials or failure probabilities.
The last three methods are intentionally unacceptable controls. Unavailability is
separately counted; it is not silently labeled incorrect arithmetic or full preservation.

The compressed method declines the initial/replayed/prior-updated diamond in 24
outcomes because its blocks partially overlap. It loses required lawful coverage after
withdrawal in another 16: a mixed block cannot isolate its surviving component. With
singleton blocks it can withdraw r2 exactly. All layouts regain coverage after the
clean rebuild. The scalar method also rejects overlap and missing coverage; another
24 states are unavailable because their prior stamps are stale. Its 56 available
answers all understate variance in this grid.

For sigma2=1, tau2=4 in the unequal disjoint layout, the correct initial x answer is
(mean 10/3, variance 13/3). The scalar children are (1,9/2) and (8,5); pretending them
independent yields (82/19,45/19). Both mean and uncertainty are wrong. In contrast,
counting the prior twice leaves mean 10/3 but changes variance to 7/3. The latter even
preserves Var(x+b)=1/3, demonstrating why one mean or one favorable query cannot stand
in for the full covariance check. A separate replay test sends identical evidence
through an extra child ID: proper composition remains unchanged, while counting priors
by child route invents still more precision.

The exact atomic event sequence for that configuration is:

| State | Mean x | Var(x) | Var(x+b) | Consecutive mean change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Initial | 10/3 | 13/3 | 1/3 | — |
| Equivalent replacement/replay | 10/3 | 13/3 | 1/3 | 0 |
| Independent calibration-prior update | 4/3 | 4/3 | 1/3 | -2 |
| Withdraw r2 | 2 | 3/2 | 1/2 | 2/3 |
| Rebuild/replay | 2 | 3/2 | 1/2 | 0 |

No smoothing is applied. The frozen control looks steady at withdrawal, staying at
4/3, while illegally retaining r2's contribution; the lineage check identifies it.
The real prior update also causes a correction without new observations. Neither a
zero jump nor the rate of new observation arrivals determines validity. Jump metrics
are undefined across unavailable states, and the mean shift is not converted into a
motion/control claim. T02's physical response requirements remain separate.

## Exact insufficiency witnesses

**Unknown dependence from marginal-only state.** Two children with means 0 and 8,
variances 5 and 5, and disjoint observation IDs can arise from independent unit noise
plus a common bias of variance 4, or from separate independent biases of variance 4.
Their observation covariance matrices are respectively [[5,4],[4,5]] and [[5,0],[0,5]].
Direct covariance algebra gives mean 4 in both cases, but variance 9/2 versus 5/2.
The marginal-only retained schema is identical, so it cannot select both correct
answers. Shared-latent identity or adequate cross-covariance is additional information;
this is not a ban on scalar summaries carrying that information.

**Known lineage and shared latent, lost overlap values.** Consider worlds:

```
             r1  r2  r3     left sum(r1,r2)   right sum(r2,r3)
world A       0   2   8            2                  10
world B       2   0  10            2                  10
```

Both children have identical lineage, counts and conditional information matrices/
vectors in the two worlds. Their full unique-data means, however, are 10/3 and 4.
Thus no function of those retained aggregates alone reconstructs the full answer in
both worlds. The representation omits likelihood constants independent of x/b, as is
usual for this five-coefficient factor; the claim is about precisely that retained
schema. It is not a universal impossibility result for richer retained state.

The two retained averages are 1 and 5. Without calibration uncertainty their known
covariance is [[1/2,1/4],[1/4,1/2]]. Computing the Gaussian answer using exactly that
reduced observation vector gives mean 3, variance 3/8. This is an honest answer for
the reduced experiment. Full raw data gives variance 1/3 and the world-specific means
above. Naive independent fusion instead gives mean 3, variance 1/4: false precision.

The difference of the two raw worlds is proportional to (1,-1,1), which both child
sums erase, but the total unique-data sum does not erase. Known cross-covariance makes
the retained averages' uncertainty correct; it cannot reconstruct that discarded
realization. A numerical overlap contribution, finer decomposition, sufficient extra
statistics or replay could change what is recoverable. The current compressed method
chooses explicit unavailability for full-data queries, rather than implementing every
possible reduced-data fallback. The honest reduced-data calculation is retained as a
separate witness so that this conservative choice is not mistaken for impossibility
of *any* useful answer.

## Design implications, limits and next action

Nestbox should distinguish observation identity, mathematical dependency, summary
identity/revision, and calibration/model identity. A versioned transport prevents
stale replacement, but does not make a returned posterior an independent likelihood.
Likewise, a lineage set detects partial overlap without supplying its numerical value.
Choose the retained representation according to supported future queries and withdrawal
partitions; do not promise arbitrary selective recovery from an opaque aggregate.

For Soradyne, this supports the existing separation: versioned artifacts, permissions,
retention/replay coverage and explicit application-provided dependency metadata belong
in the relevant generic contracts. Their solver meaning stays application-owned.
Soradyne must not infer shared Gaussian variables or decomposability from opaque bytes.
This cycle does not define how human withdrawal intent becomes a complete derivative
policy, bind untrusted artifacts to their claimed lineage, or combine cycle 0004's
entitlement engine with this representation. Mandatory R04+I03 remains unexecuted.

Evidence is exact for this declared linear Gaussian model and its two insufficiency
witnesses. It is not statistical calibration evidence, a nonlinear or floating-point
stability result, a proof for arbitrary nested eliminations, or a physical-safety
claim. Only leaf-to-child-to-parent composition and trusted local snapshots are tested.
Retention erasure, online prefixes, concurrent policies, malicious evidence, crashes,
latency and growing archives remain outside this slice. Full I03 is not closed.

Next bounded cycle: I04 feedback/repeated influence in a tiny exact scalar or two-variable
message sequence. Compare a restrictive supported message flow, explicit original-
evidence accounting, and a negative control that feeds a parent posterior back as
independent evidence under new packet revisions/paths. The oracle must count original
observations once, not infer independence from an acyclic container graph. Exercise
repetition without new observations and a withdrawal followed by stale returned state;
measure covariance, lineage, availability and estimate jumps together. Keep general
I05 gauge work and nonlinear/recovery expansion queued.
