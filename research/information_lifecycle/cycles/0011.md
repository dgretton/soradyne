## Findings

The held-out measurements confirm undercoverage in the weakest geometry. Intervals
labeled nominally 95% covered the true angle only **88.84–89.65%** of the time for the
local observed-Hessian approximation and **89.23–90.16%** for Gauss–Newton. The full
circular likelihood's intervals covered **94.48–95.34%** there. The latter are compatible
with 95% under the predeclared simultaneous confidence test; the former are not.

The full intervals are broad: their median half-width in the weak cells is about
**114.5–115.7 degrees**. Correctly representing uncertainty does not make the pose
accurate or adequate for a moving consumer. All three nonlinear methods have the same
point estimate; their different intervals describe that estimate differently.

A frozen approximation can look calibrated when tested at a truth equal to its anchor.
The zero-anchor method has about 95% coverage at true angle 0, but only 15.5% in the
held-out weak cells at true angle 170 degrees. Its anchor is fixed before sampling;
no estimator is given truth. This exposes the importance of varying geometry and
orientation in validation rather than choosing only favorable initialization cases.

For this particular model, the full interval's coverage also has an exact algebraic
justification. The experiment tests an implementation of that calculation. The result
does not validate generic Bayesian intervals, general frozen survey priors, joint SE(2)
confidence, unknown noise, latent calibration, distributed authorization or hardware.

## Model, methods and prediction

Use the four known body points (-1,-.5),(-1,.5),(1,-.5),(1,.5), scaled by 1 or .1,
with centroid offset (0,0) or (3,-2). Measurements have independent isotropic Gaussian
noise with known sigma .05 or .2 m. True orientation is fixed at 0 or 170 degrees for
each cell; translation is (.3,-.2) m. There are 16 cells. The datum, scale and
correspondences are known. No landmarks or calibration variables are estimated or
eliminated. All four IDs and raw/derived representations retain explicit P0 rights.

Inference receives points, measurements, sigma and a predetermined anchor where needed.
Truth is used only for generation and evaluation. Additive chart [theta,t_x,t_y] uses
radians/metres; the retained angular variance is the marginal after translation is
accounted for, not an isolated Hessian diagonal with cross terms ignored. Conventions
and application references remain at nestbox-ng `041f1146df2e2a271cd0489fda520bc65f2e9f70`.
No application implementation or GTSAM is imported.

For centered p, let S=sum||p'||^2. The nonlinear summary's dot/cross statistics obey

    (a,b) ~ N(S*[cos(theta),sin(theta)], sigma^2*S*I).

Their Gaussian noise components have zero cross-covariance; centering removes the
unknown translation. Write rho=hypot(a,b) and alpha=atan2(b,a). Changing to polar
coordinates gives the conditional sampling density

    p(alpha-theta | rho) proportional to exp(kappa*cos(alpha-theta)),
    kappa = rho/sigma^2.

The radial density is independent of true orientation. Therefore the symmetric arc
whose conditional mass is .95 contains the true angle with probability .95 at every
fixed orientation, conditional on any positive rho. This agrees with the angular
posterior under uniform angle and flat translation priors. The equality follows from
this model's symmetry and known noise; it is not a general property of credible sets.
Rho=0 has probability zero under these continuous, nondegenerate fixtures; numerical
rank failure is treated as a loud failure, never a dropped trial.

Six methods consume each dataset:

| Method | Pose estimate | Angular interval |
|---|---|---|
| Full arc | Nonlinear moment optimum | Symmetric 95% arc of the full circular likelihood |
| Observed Gaussian | Same optimum | +/-1.96*sqrt(sigma^2/rho), capped at the whole circle |
| Gauss–Newton | Same optimum | +/-1.96*sqrt(sigma^2/S), capped at the whole circle |
| Frozen 0 | First-order residual at angle 0, translation 0 | Its Gaussian angular interval |
| Frozen−30 | First-order residual at −30 degrees, translation 0 | Its Gaussian angular interval |
| Frozen−90 | First-order residual at −90 degrees, translation 0 | Its Gaussian angular interval |

The caps prevent intervals larger than a circle. They do not fix undercoverage of the
local approximations. Observed-Gaussian intervals cover the whole circle in 1,031 of
the 32,768 weak-cell datasets, yet undercoverage remains in every weak cell.

The independent raw reference uses a batched determinant-corrected SVD. All 131,072
moment poses agree with it within the 1e-9 numerical threshold; maximum absolute pose
component discrepancy is 1.83e-14. Curvature disagreement is at most 2.53e-14 relative.
At the first eight trials in each of 32 cells, scalar implementations from 0010 verify
pose and angular variances, and adaptive integration of raw residuals checks the full
arc's mass. Maximum scalar pose difference is 1.23e-12, angular-variance relative
difference 1.73e-12, raw-integrated mass error 3.34e-15. These 256 audits are separate from
the batched all-trial checks. The largest reported adaptive-integration error estimate
is 3.67e-10, below the declared 1e-8 numerical requirement.

## Anchor dependence, probability and dynamic interpretation

Conditional posterior mass and fixed-truth coverage are distinct quantities. In the
same weak centered held-out angle 0 cell, frozen_0 has 94.995% empirical coverage but
only about 85.33% mean posterior mass inside its reported arc. At angle 170 degrees its
empirical coverage is 15.50%, while mean posterior mass is about 53.09%. The latter
diagnostic uses the library's approximate CDF, explicitly labeled in the data; the
large differences here are not explained by its small numerical approximation.

A post-run algebraic explanation, not a tuned replacement candidate: the unwrapped
frozen angle has Gaussian sampling mean theta0+sin(theta−theta0) and variance sigma^2/S.
For theta0=0, S=.05 and sigma=.2, summing the normal probability over the wrapped arc
predicts coverage about .950000409 at theta=0 and .148466480 at theta=170 degrees. The
favorable anchor makes a one-orientation test look good; it does not establish uniform
coverage. The exact exploratory calculation is reproducible as follows:

```sh
/Users/rim/Dev/.venv-nestbox/bin/python -B - <<'PY'
import math
from scipy.stats import norm
sd=math.sqrt(.8)
h=1.959963984540054*sd
for theta in (0,math.radians(170)):
    mu=math.sin(theta)
    p=sum(norm.cdf((theta+2*math.pi*k+h-mu)/sd)-norm.cdf((theta+2*math.pi*k-h-mu)/sd) for k in range(-4,5))
    print(theta,p)
PY
```

The omitted normal tails beyond these nine wrapped copies are negligible at this sd;
this calculation is explanatory, not another Monte Carlo validation cell.

Each representative trace publishes at tick0, repeats at tick1 and rebuilds at tick2
using the same four original measurements. Full/nonlinear methods keep their uncertainty
convention; frozen methods rebuild to the raw nonlinear mode with Gauss–Newton width.
New-observation count remains zero and all represented IDs retain P0 permission. The
largest compact-summary/raw rebuild discrepancy is 3.64e-14 m at the queried points.
The largest frozen correction is 16.55 m in deliberately extreme/weak fixtures. These
are model/query errors and publication changes, not measured motion or physical failures.

The compact estimate can itself be inaccurate: maximum truth-relative query error in
this Gaussian sample is 10.24 m for the offset/weak configurations. Its correctly broad
angular interval is useful information about that limitation. No consumer can infer
usable precision merely from an available estimate or a calibrated 95% label.

## Numerical correction and validation

The initial 16-test run failed one independent quadrature check. At kappa=100, inversion
of SciPy 1.17.1's von Mises CDF returned h=.19655905677406504 radians, whose independently
integrated mass is .949998597154059: error 1.40285e-6 exceeds our declared 1e-7 requirement.
This is a numerical-accuracy limitation relative to this experiment's tolerance, not
an architectural contradiction or a claim that the library promises greater accuracy.

The retained `library_arc_half_width` records that attempt. The accepted implementation
uses 50 bounded bisections of a 64-point Gauss–Legendre integral, normalized with the
scaled Bessel function i0e; a 32-point rule checks the final result independently of the
64-point rule. The bracket is checked explicitly. At kappa=100 the corrected h is
.19656026425814202 with raw mass .9500000000000003. The largest mass error across the
seven declared concentration checks is 1.12e-15. Raw adaptive integration supplies a
further independent check; inverse/CDF agreement alone would have missed the defect.

This correction occurred before formal primary and held-out execution. Numerical
thresholds and statistical design were unchanged. Primary-only 64-trial smoke fixtures
had already been used for numerical checks. Ruff import ordering, a dict literal and
a loop-variable closure style warning were also corrected. No statistical result was
used to tune the method or choose another seed.

**200 tests pass**, including 16 new tests; Ruff passes. Python 3.13.4, NumPy 1.26.4,
SciPy 1.17.1 in the existing environment. No packages installed. Primary execution took

## Implications, limits and next slice

For Nestbox, a pose plus local covariance needs an explicit approximation contract.
Tests should include weak geometry and orientations away from the freeze anchor, with
truth-based repeated-sample checks separate from posterior mass. In this primitive model,
a retained nonlinear statistic and a circular interval give a simple, justified answer;
weak data still yield a broad answer that a precision-demanding query may need to decline.
No threshold for that consumer decision is established here. Angular calibration alone
does not calibrate point-position, translation or joint pose confidence.

For Soradyne, representation/revision identity and permitted retained-state contracts
remain appropriate. Numerical meaning belongs to the application. This cycle introduces
no protocol schema, authorization change or numerical responsibility for the provider.
T01/T02 gain evidence; no new architectural contradiction is established. I06 remains
partial, and no family or overall investigation is complete.

Next bounded chunk 0012: freeze a **converged** first batch, then add a separate independent
measurement batch of the same static pose. Compare a retained first-order quadratic,
the exact nonlinear moment summary and all-raw recomputation as the optimum moves.
Predeclare the two batches' geometry/noise, information ratios and bounded perturbations;
distinguish valid updates from approximation error, retaining input identity and covariance
checks. This addresses the prescribed-poor-anchor limitation of 0010–0011. Unknown landmark/
calibration elimination follows after that baseline; the present moment formula is not
assumed sufficient there. SE(3), asynchronous faults and physical consumers stay queued.
