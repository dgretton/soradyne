## Findings

The unit of retained decomposition matters. A separate summary for each independent
record supports removing any one record in this model. A summary for each source
supports removing an entire source, but cannot generally support removing just one
record within that source after its raw history has expired. One mixed summary needs
reconstruction for either kind of withdrawal. These statements concern the specified
scalar sufficient statistics, not arbitrary nonlinear marginalized state.

When reconstruction is needed, starting replay before raw expiry is insufficient.
Under this fixture's read-at-completion contract, withdrawal time W and replay delay D
must satisfy W+D<E, where E is raw expiry. Equality is too late. A request does not pin
records or preserve a hidden copy. Once the necessary raw values are gone, an input
manifest names the missing information but does not recreate it.

The two-world witness makes this an information limit, rather than just a failed
implementation: identical retained source/mixed aggregates and identity-only manifests
require different answers after the same record ID is withdrawn. An algorithm receiving
only that state cannot select the right answer in both worlds.

There is also a useful middle course between a complete answer and total unavailability:
an explicitly reduced answer, computed from and labeled with the actual permitted
subset. It has a different mean/uncertainty and must not satisfy a caller requiring the
original complete history. The reduced candidate is correct for its declared subset in
all 2,496 checks; silently calling those same numbers complete fails 1,048 checks.

The findings strengthen the existing retention/withdrawal tension T01 and response
contract T02. They do not establish a new architectural contradiction. They also do
not establish that a consumer can safely continue on a reduced answer.

## Model and independent reference

One dimensionless static x. Independent Gaussian observations, with permanent datum
d=0, variance 1. At t=0 acquire a1=0,a2=10,b1=-4,b2=4. The equal-value fixture sets all
values to zero. Variance pairs (Va,Vb) are (1,1) or (4,1), shared by each source's two
independent observations; there is no correlated source noise here. At t=8 acquire a
new permitted n=-8 (zero in the equal fixture), variance 1.

For a requested unique set S, the independent reference normalizes inverse-variance
weights: mean=sum(z_i*(1/v_i)/sum(1/v_j)), variance=1/sum(1/v_i). The candidate instead
sums retained information pairs (H,eta) and returns (eta/H,1/H). Exact Fractions avoid
fitted tolerances. An evaluator separately tracks original input coefficients and checks
that they reconstruct each numerical H/eta, including partial and deliberately forbidden
state. These audit coefficients never drive candidate admission or fusion.

The strict query requires d and every still-permitted historical record, plus n after
arrival. Storage expiry does not silently remove an input from that requested coverage.
The reduced contract may publish the exact posterior on its named surviving subset,
with reduced status and missing IDs. Both the subset reference and the difference from
the full reference are recorded. The latter is a difference between conditional answers,
not an empirical bias or ground-truth error estimate. No Monte Carlo coverage is claimed.

At t=1, raw records leave active computation and are frozen. The independent archive
retains raw records until E in {3,6}; historical summary use expires at C in {7,11}.
This slice intentionally has E<C. Both intervals are half-open: t>=deadline is too late.
Raw expiry is a P2-style case with explicitly surviving derivative rights. It does not
mean a contribution has been withdrawn. The d and n summaries have deadline 16, beyond
the run. The archive clears its own lookup state at E; evaluator data remain outside
candidate access for reproducibility. No physical/remote erasure is claimed.

All reconstructed derivatives of these historical inputs share the original deadline C.
Changing a summary's name or creation time cannot extend that right in this fixture.
This per-input cap is an explicit policy choice within one authorized use, not a proposed
universal rule for every owner, audience or independent grant. Explicitly authorized
renewal or request-time retention pins would require different fixtures.

## Withdrawal, replay and timeline

P3 at W in {2,3,6} removes either a1 or both A records from future inference. Current
permissions invalidate affected raw and derived inputs immediately. Remaining raw replay
is authorized while retained. Effective, learned and enforced policy times coincide;
authentication, complete policy metadata and local enforcement are trusted assumptions.
This does not exercise offline authority discovery or distributed revocation.

Query at every integer t=0..12. Each tick applies archive/artifact expiry, then P3,
then due replay, then n if t=8. Replay delay D is 0 or 2 ticks. The archive reads at
completion, not request initiation. A failed replay receives no later miraculous copy.
Successful reconstruction deduplicates deliveries and covers only missing permitted
IDs. At t=12 an external delivery copy of any rebuilt summary is offered again with
its original deadline and is rejected as expired.

For D=0, invalidation and a lawful complete rebuild can happen before that tick's query;
the old mixed artifact is still removed before the rebuild. Pending replay never acts
as permission to retain forbidden numerical state. Observation acquisition, policy,
replay request/completion and publication times are recorded separately. Ticks are
synthetic time units, not measured network/CPU latency bounds.

Two value fixtures x two variance pairs x two E x two C x three W x two D x two scopes
= 192 configurations per method. Six methods produce 1,152 runs and 14,976 query outcomes.
No randomness, physical plant or smoothing is used.

## Methods and outcomes

- `atomic`: four independent record summaries; drop only the withdrawn entries.
- `by_source`: two summaries, A and B; invalidate any group intersecting P3 and replay
  the missing permitted component when possible.
- `mixed`: one A+B summary; invalidate it on either withdrawal, then attempt replay.
- `honest_subset`: same state and replay policy as by_source, but declare reduced
  coverage when the strict requested history cannot be reconstructed.
- `coarse_complete`: negative control, same state/arithmetic as by_source, silently
  label every subset result complete.
- `hold_mixed`: negative control, keep the mixed artifact despite P3 until its normal
  expiry and always claim complete coverage. Ordinary expiry still works in this control.

All methods retain d separately and add n once. A single-record scalar summary is
raw-equivalent information here; it is not a privacy reduction. Retention/use rights
are explicitly granted. The supported methods neither subtract a now-forbidden raw
record nor infer permission from algebraic cancellation.

Each method has 2,496 time-query checks. Counts overlap; they are not probabilities.

| Method | Declared complete | Declared reduced | Unavailable | Wrong complete claims | Forbidden processing/output |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Per-record summaries | 1,728 | 0 | 768 | 0 | 0 |
| Per-source summaries | 1,448 | 0 | 1,048 | 0 | 0 |
| One mixed summary | 1,168 | 0 | 1,328 | 0 | 0 |
| Explicit permitted subset | 1,448 | 1,048 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Subset silently called complete | 2,496 | 0 | 0 | 1,048 | 0 |

The three strict methods pass all 7,488 combined checks: available answers match the
full reference exactly, and missing required coverage produces unavailability. The subset
candidate additionally passes all its own reference/permission/coverage-label checks.
Every method's arithmetic matches its actual represented set, including the negatives.
Arithmetic correctness alone therefore misses the forbidden-use and coverage failures.

Before artifact expiry, full recovery occurs in 192/192 atomic runs, 136/192 by-source
runs, and 80/192 mixed runs. By-source has 96 whole-A cases needing no replay, plus
40 successful single-record rebuilds. Mixed has 80 successful rebuilds across both
scopes. Where replay is required, completion before E exactly predicts success in
the declared grid. Per-record and whole-source-compatible representations recover
immediately at W. Successful delayed replay takes two ticks; otherwise no full recovery
occurs before C. At C, all strict methods become unavailable for the full historical
query, even though a permitted smaller answer may still exist.

## Concrete witnesses and dynamic behavior

For unit variances, the full initial answer is (mean 2, variance 1/5). If a1 is withdrawn
at t=3 exactly when the raw archive expires, the required result is (5/2,1/4). Atomic
summaries supply it. The source summary A cannot separate a2, so a strict by-source
answer becomes unavailable; its missing set is {a2}. The lawful reduced answer uses
d,b1,b2 and is (0,1/3), one unit of precision less than the full target. Silently labeling
it complete is wrong even though its own subset covariance is correct.

After n arrives, the full answer is (2/5,1/5), while that reduced answer becomes
(-2,1/4). Once historical summaries expire at t=11, the remaining d,n answer is
(-4,1/2), with a2,b1,b2 explicitly missing. A consumer may or may not accept that
weaker input contract. Neither the number's existence nor smoothness establishes its
suitability for a task.

If instead both A records are withdrawn, the source-group representation immediately
produces the correct (0,1/3), without replay even after raw expiry. The mixed representation
still needs the surviving B values. This is why retaining more independent groups can
help, and why the required granularity follows the promised withdrawal operations.

For the insufficiency witness, compare two worlds with A values (0,10) and (2,8), unit
variances, identical B and d, identical IDs, creation times and expiry times. Both have
A information pair (2,10), B pair (2,0), mixed pair (4,10), and the same initial answer
(2,1/5). After withdrawing a1, their required answers are respectively (5/2,1/4) and
(2,1/4). The retained aggregate plus identity-only manifest is identical. This proves
insufficiency of that representation when the withdrawn ID, rather than an additional
authorized numerical contribution, is supplied. Richer state, a different permitted
removal operation, or still-available replay changes the premises.

The equal-value fixtures expose forbidden use without mean movement. hold_mixed has
zero withdrawal jump while still using a1/a2; its false precision and lineage fail the
checks. Correct reduced or complete answers accept the new n immediately; they do not
blend with a revoked value to preserve continuity. Adjacent-valid mean jumps and total
variation are saved, but a gap makes a jump undefined. Expiry can change a declared
subset without a new acquisition; that is a coverage event, not new measurement evidence.
No consumer motion or safety conclusion follows from these exact inference traces.

## Prototype corrections and validation

The initial 19-test run had two failures found by admission review:

1. `test_late_relabel_cannot_renew_retention_permission`: expected
   `reject_deadline_change`, got `accepted`. Admission trusted an artifact's new expiry
   without binding it to the already authorized historical-input deadline.
2. `test_future_artifact_is_not_available_yet`: expected `reject_future`, got `accepted`.
   Admission checked expiry but omitted the artifact creation-time lower bound.

The engine now keeps permitted derivative deadlines as identity metadata, checks them
before admission, and rejects future-created artifacts. A further regression prevents
reusing an old source ID as a purported new acquisition to renew its deadline. These
are prototype validation defects, not new architecture tensions. The exact 1,152 run
metrics were saved before the fixes and compared afterward: all are unchanged, since
the original grid used well-formed artifact metadata. No acceptance threshold was relaxed.
The failing tests are retained; their names identify direct reproducers for removing
the respective guards. Lint also found two unused test imports, which were removed.

Final boundary review added `test_rejected_new_acquisition_does_not_mutate_retained_metadata`.
It failed because an over-capacity new acquisition changed the target/deadline metadata
before raising its validation error. The engine now validates capacity and constructs
the bounded-lifetime block before committing metadata. The regression also checks a
new acquisition after that lifetime's deadline; rejection leaves the state unchanged.
All run metrics remain identical to those saved before the admission corrections.

## Resource accounting and reproducibility

The common pre-freeze state has five entries including d, the peak committed
representation size for every method in this grid. Immediately after freezing, atomic
has five entries, source grouping three and mixed grouping two. There are two numerical
payload coefficients per entry. Over [0,12), retained-entry integrals range from 34-55
entry-ticks for atomic, 27-40 for source methods, 21-30 for mixed, and 26-30 for hold.
The separate raw archive uses exactly 4E record-ticks (12 or 24). These are structural
counts, excluding metadata, construction scratch, evaluator histories and physical byte
sizes. They do not prove that an aggregate is cheaper in a real implementation.
Grouped summaries still retain per-input identity metadata; this is not a constant-memory
solution for an unbounded history.

Bounds are 16 source/retained entries, 32 logical times and eight replay records per
batch; this fixed suite uses six source IDs, 13 times, four archive records and one
pending request at most. Repeated delivery neither increases precision nor renews the
artifact's permission. Stored evidence is 1,325,432 bytes: all run metrics, the two-world

## Implications, limits and next action

For Nestbox, specify which withdrawal units each frozen representation supports and
what replay/retention assumptions make the others reconstructible. Exact covariance
for today's marginal does not promise tomorrow's removal operations. Where individual
withdrawal is required after raw expiry, preserve appropriately separable authorized
state or explicitly decline that guarantee. Define whether a query requires full
requested history or accepts a labeled subset; a global available/unavailable flag
alone does not explain the coverage/uncertainty change.

For Soradyne, retention rights, replay completion semantics, remaining entitlements and
artifact deadlines need explicit generic contracts. A request must not implicitly pin
data or renew a derivative if its grants do not authorize that behavior. The provider
can preserve identities and enforce declared rights while leaving sufficient statistics
and query adequacy to the application. No core schema or application interface was
changed here. Other owners' archives and independent authorizations are outside this
one-use fixture and are not made subject to its deadline by implication.

This is the first dedicated bounded R03+F03 compound witness. It does not close the
whole compound family: the state is static and scalar, without correlated noise,
nonlinear marginalization, moving-state lag elimination, floating-point sensitivity,
asynchronous policy discovery, production storage or real replay latency. C>E and the
no-pin/no-renewal policies are explicit tested choices, not universal requirements.
Reduced uncertainty is exact for the declared subset under the independent model;
consumer acceptability and physical stability remain untested.

Next bounded slice: I06's first nonlinear freeze case. Use a small SE(2) pose/landmark
model, a frozen linearization, controlled angular displacement from that linearization
and a full-data batch reference. Predeclare geometry/noise, a bounded sweep and independent
residual/error/uncertainty checks. Distinguish approximation error from lost information
or permissions. Start with deterministic geometry; add fixed-seed coverage only after
the independent reference is validated and within the cycle budget. This follows the
existing plan after the initial recovery/retention witnesses, not a claim that those
families or real-time consumer behavior are complete.
