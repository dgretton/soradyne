## Main findings

Replacing a child result by its newest revision does not prevent feedback from
recounting the parent's evidence. Nor does an acyclic graph of immutable, versioned
artifacts. In the exact negative control, twelve feedback rounds without new observations
leave the mean unchanged but claim thirteen times the justified precision. A real new
observation then produces a much smaller correction than the unique-data model requires.
This is a concrete mechanism by which smooth-looking repeated corrections can damage
later responsiveness; it is not a physical-safety simulation.

Three supported approaches agree exactly with the unique-evidence reference in all
1,824 of their combined event/query checks:

1. Keep the upstream contract to local likelihoods. Reject a returned parent-conditioned
   posterior; retain the already admitted, still-authorized local evidence.
2. Carry separable original contributions and count each observation once, including
   when a message returns through another route.
3. In this additive scalar model only, subtract the exact retained conditioning base
   used to build the child posterior, and replace the child's local contribution.

The first is simplest here. The third needs more retained state and a stronger
contract; this fixture gives no reason to prefer it when the child can export its
local likelihood directly. Subtracting the current parent instead of the actual old
conditioning parent can silently cancel a legitimate new observation, even while the
residual has positive precision. These conclusions do not extend residual subtraction
to marginalized or nonlinear models.

Strict withdrawal needs the dependencies of returned state, not only the direct input
list. The full-lineage negative control correctly becomes unavailable when its mixed
posterior is forbidden. The direct-only control continues using the withdrawn input
through that posterior, including cases with no visible mean change. Clean replay
restores the proper answer, but incorrect feedback can immediately corrupt it again.

No new architectural contradiction was established. The results strengthen existing
information-preservation and consumer-response concerns, with explicit representation
and permission assumptions.

## Model and independent checks

An unknown dimensionless scalar x has a flat prior. Independent observations a,b,c
have fixed values (0,10,-8) in the contrast fixture, and (0,0,0) in the equal fixture.
Variances of a and b each range over {1,4}; variance(c)=4. The parent initially has a,
and a child supplies b as a local likelihood. Only c is newly acquired later. Repeating
b in a reply never means another measurement of b.

The grid is two value fixtures, four variance pairs, two return paths (direct or a
transparent relay), and K in {1,4,12} feedback rounds: 48 configurations per method,
240 runs. The relay changes artifact identity but supplies no observation; it preserves
the original conditioning-base reference. Fractions make equality exact. There is no
randomness, fitted tolerance, statistical coverage measurement or hidden shared noise.

The independent reference normalizes inverse-variance weights over the unique currently
required original observations. The candidate composes scalar information pairs
(precision, precision-times-mean). The evaluator separately propagates coefficients of
original contributions through every numerical operation. Those coefficient maps are
never given to candidate admission or fusion methods. At every query, reconstructing
the candidate's information from the diagnostic coefficients must match its actual
numerical state. Mean, variance, declared lineage and actual influence are all checked.

A message identifies its kind, stable child revision, model/boundary, complete source
lineage, local source b, original conditioning base, and artifact dependencies. The
components candidate additionally receives individually separable original factors.
Scalar candidates do not receive those factors. Authentication, complete metadata and
the declared formula Q=P_sent+L_b are trusted premises; the prototype does not certify
those semantic assertions from an arbitrary opaque payload or a signature.

The artifact graph is a graph of immutable computation versions, not a static graph
forbidding the parent and child from communicating both ways. Every artifact depends
only on already-existing artifacts; a separate rank check confirms acyclicity in all
runs. A strict contract prohibiting returned influence can work, as local_only shows.
The failing property is assuming that acyclic artifact references themselves establish
statistical independence. No generic ban on shared-flow references is proposed, and
the retired Alloy restrictions are not reintroduced.

## Timeline, rights and availability

Each run has K+7 query states, 3,040 outcomes total:

- Initialize a and local b. Perform K feedback rounds, each returning P_sent+L_b at a
  newer revision, with a duplicate and late replay of local revision 1.
- Prepare another reply, authorize/acquire c, then deliver the delayed reply. Correct
  residual extraction uses its retained old base, not the parent now containing c.
- Prepare a pending reply. Withdraw a under P3 and query immediately. Deliver that
  now-contaminated reply, a duplicate, and old local revision 1; query again.
- Replay authorized clean local b at a newer revision. Finally return another clean
  parent-conditioned posterior, testing whether the bad feedback rule resumes.

Policy versions are 1 for {a,b}, 2 for {a,b,c}, and 3 for {b,c}. Authorization of c
and its acquisition share one fixture event. Event indices, observation-acquisition
indices and policy effective/learned/enforced indices are recorded separately. Policy
installation is synchronous, so those three policy indices coincide; delayed messages
still point to the older conditioning artifact. Logical event indices are not wall
clock delays or an online revocation-discovery guarantee.

The three supported methods retain valid b and therefore stay available. local_only
rejects feedback without throwing away that prior admitted likelihood. components
can filter forbidden a out of a separable old message. bound_residual retains its
already-extracted b factor, but rejects a newly arriving mixed reply BEFORE subtraction
after P3. It also drops forbidden sent-parent bases. Missing or mismatched bases do
not authorize an approximate substitution. Unit tests cover an empty receiver that
must report unavailable when its only b-bearing message cannot be admitted.

Retention/use rights for independent b components and the previously extracted exact
b residual are explicit assumptions here. The residual's numerical dependence on the
parent was canceled before withdrawal; its historical causal ancestry is not erased.
Mathematical cancellation alone does not grant permission to retain or reuse a
particular derivative. A policy forbidding that artifact despite cancellation would
need a different availability result. These fixtures do not settle that product policy
or the translation of withdrawal intent into derivative entitlements. Revision history
and evaluator/causal diagnostics are not claimed physically erased.

The full-lineage posterior control invalidates its active mixed result and rejects the
late mixed reply. Its remaining local c cannot satisfy required coverage {b,c}, so it
withholds the answer until clean b arrives. The direct-only control removes a locally
but preserves its nonzero influence inside the returned posterior. A new version does
not make that influence newly authorized.

## Additional exact witnesses

**A stale conditioning base can produce a plausible wrong residual.** With unit-variance
a=0 and b=10, P_old has pair (2,10). The child reply Q=P_old+B is (3,20). New c=-8 with
variance 4 changes the parent to P_current=(9/4,8). Matching the base yields B=(1,10).
Subtracting the current parent instead yields (3/4,12), a proper scalar Gaussian with
mean 16 and variance 4/3. Numerically it is B-C, not B. Adding it to the parent's A+C
silently discards c and returns the obsolete answer (5,1/2), instead of (32/9,4/9).

Positive precision alone does not validate this operation. In the b-variance-4 corner,
the wrong residual instead has zero precision and nonzero information (0,9/2), which
cannot be published as a proper Gaussian. The supported method rejects nonproper
residuals, absent bases, inconsistent base metadata and model/boundary mismatch.
It still trusts the child's additive decomposition; more general elimination or
linearization requires a different representation and proof.

**A bounded iteration can converge to the wrong answer.** The separately retained
negative control applies P_next=A+(P_previous+B)/4 for twelve no-data rounds. Its
original-information weights satisfy a direct, independently checked recurrence:

```
alpha = 1/4
weight_a(k) = (1-alpha^(k+1))/(1-alpha)
weight_b(k) = alpha^k + alpha*(1-alpha^k)/(1-alpha)
```

They converge to 4/3 and 1/3, hence mean 2 and reported variance 3/5. The required
unique-data answer stays (5,1/2). The mean approaches 2 monotonically, within 10^-6
by round 12. This particular damping rule gives a stable-looking but incorrect answer;
it is not evidence against all valid iterative or damping methods, and does not claim
a population bias estimate from these fixed synthetic observations.

## Implications, limits and next action

For Nestbox, specify whether an upstream message is local evidence, a conditional
boundary factor, or a posterior already containing external influence. These have
different composition rules. A changed revision/path is not a new observation, and a
converged estimate or small correction is not sufficient evidence of correct uncertainty.
Where feedback is necessary, explicit original-information accounting or a justified
model-specific message calculation must replace assumed independence.

For Soradyne, preserve immutable artifact/configuration identity and requested replay
coverage, while keeping numerical meaning in the application. Authentic routing and
revision replacement cannot certify that a claimed local factor excludes a parent
prior. Generic lifecycle/retention support can carry the identities needed for a bound
calculation; the provider should not subtract solver information or infer it from bytes.
No stronger shared-flow topology restriction follows from this experiment.

The supported results are exact for this fixed scalar additive model, trusted metadata
and explicit derivative rights. They do not establish general loopy inference, nonlinear
residual subtraction, numerical conditioning under floating-point cancellation, hostile
provenance validation, delayed authority discovery, crashes or physical stability.
Full I04 and the mandatory compound cases remain open. Retained-state recovery here
is one clean replay, not the F-family recovery program.

Next bounded cycle: I05, with a narrow R06 anchor-loss case. Test a tiny exact linear
system in which losing the last absolute anchor leaves a relative query observable.
Compare explicit observable/unobservable answers with blanket invalidation and an
artificial fixed gauge that could fabricate absolute precision. Include consistent
coordinate-origin changes, reference covariance for relative queries, and immediate
withdrawal of anchor influence. Keep broader floating-point/nonlinear geometry,
consumer dynamics and recovery work queued.
