## Main findings

Validity belongs to the requested quantity. Removing the last absolute anchor makes
absolute position unavailable in this model, but leaves the measured separation and
its uncertainty unchanged. Removing the relative constraint instead leaves the anchored
coordinate usable. Rejecting every query because the full joint matrix is singular
unnecessarily discards information. Pinning an arbitrary coordinate and reporting its
conditional covariance as external-position uncertainty invents information.

Two simple candidates agree exactly with the independent reference in all 1,920 of
their combined event-query checks: retain permitted factors and test each query's
observable subspace; or retain the independently authorized absolute and relative scalar
summaries separately. The second is sufficient only because this fixture supplies an
independent decomposition before freezing. It does not undo a general marginalization,
reconstruct discarded cross-covariance, or settle derivative permissions.

The negative controls isolate three distinct failures: 168 unnecessarily withheld
answers under blanket invalidation; 432 invented finite answers under gauge pinning;
and 480 queries that process a withdrawn anchor under the deliberate retention bug.
Some forbidden computations return the correct relative number because the anchor
cancels algebraically. Numerical correctness cannot grant permission to process it.

These are exact-model conclusions and deterministic software evidence. No new
architectural contradiction is established. The findings refine T01/T02: preserve
the lawful information needed by a query, and make loss of validity explicit before
deciding whether a consumer can continue. Physical continuation is not tested here.

## Model, reference and calculation

Unknown dimensionless coordinates x and y share one external reference. Independent
evidence A0 observes x=5 with variance V; R observes y-x=3 with variance D. Priors are
flat in directions without evidence. A later new authorized anchor A1 observes x=-2
with variance V/4 and supersedes A0. It does not reauthorize A0's history.

The independent reference works in u=x, v=y-x. The supported scalar query is
q_x*x+q_y*y=(q_x+q_y)*u+q_y*v. With both factors and coordinate origin offset t:

```
mean     = (q_x+q_y)*(a-t) + 3*q_y
variance = (q_x+q_y)^2*V + q_y^2*D
```

Substitute A1's mean and variance when it replaces A0. If a component is absent and
its coefficient is nonzero, the query has no finite posterior; otherwise that missing
component is irrelevant. The constant-zero query is separately tested as deterministic,
with variance zero and no source processing even when no evidence remains.

The rank candidate assembles H and eta from currently permitted factors and solves
H*w=q by exact rational elimination. A solution exists exactly when q is in H's range.
For this positive-semidefinite linear model, eta is also in that range. Any two solutions
differ by a null vector, orthogonal to both q and eta, so q^T*w and eta^T*w are unique.
Those are the scalar variance and mean. A free coordinate in this auxiliary calculation
is not a known physical coordinate. The u/v calculation independently checks these
answers; a separate rank-one factor on 2x+3y checks non-axis-aligned queries and scales.

When both factors exist, the joint covariance is [[V,V],[V,V+D]]. Consequently
Var(y-x)=V+(V+D)-2V=D, even for V=10^12. Omitting the covariance term would instead
report 2V+D. Weak absolute observability and strong relative observability can coexist.
The test uses exact fractions; it does not establish that subtracting huge floating-
point covariance entries is a numerically stable production implementation. An anchor
with large finite variance is mathematically observable; task-specific usefulness is
a separate decision, not a hidden rank tolerance in this experiment.

## Timeline, candidates and rights

The grid has V in {1,100,10^12}, D in {1/4,4}, origin shift t in {0,1000}, and arbitrary
gauge g in {0,7}: 24 configurations. Five queries are x, y, y-x, (x+y)/2 and -2(y-x).
Five methods each process eight states: 120 runs, 4,800 outcomes. There is no randomness.

1. Admit A0 and R at policy 1.
2. Re-express both coordinates at origin t; change the frame epoch, not the evidence.
3. Withdraw A0 under P3 at policy 2, keeping R.
4. Change the numerical gauge from g to g+11 with no new evidence.
5. Deliver withdrawn A0 twice; current policy must prevent revival.
6. Authorize and deliver A1 at policy 3 and anchor revision 2, then replay old A0.
7. Withdraw R at policy 4, keeping A1.
8. Withdraw A1 at policy 5, leaving no evidence.

The rank candidate rebuilds from allowed records. The split-summary candidate retains
only two scalar mean/variance summaries and their identities/revisions, with explicit
rights to those derivatives. It discards raw Observation objects. Its independent u/v
calculation needs only summaries with nonzero query coefficients. Frozen state is still
information requiring authorization; these single-observation summaries are not a privacy
reduction and their numerical values remain raw-equivalent in this small fixture.

The conservative control requires full rank before answering. The gauge control fixes
free coordinates and treats them as certain in the external frame. The retention control
uses the rank algorithm but ignores anchor withdrawal until a newer anchor replaces it;
it does respect R's withdrawal. These controls are intentionally incorrect alternatives,
not implementations being proposed for production.

Source identities, complete permissions and derivative rights are trusted inputs.
P3 here explicitly forbids further inference processing of the withdrawn factor. The
retention control therefore violates policy when it assembles H from that factor even
if the particular query cancels its numerical contribution, or returns unavailable.
`forbidden_output` counts an available result produced using such forbidden processing;
it is not a claim that every such result numerically depends on the anchor. Other
product policies could authorize a previously extracted derivative. Algebra alone does
not decide those rights. Historical trace diagnostics and revision hashes are not claimed
erased; no cryptographic or remote-deletion guarantee is tested.

Effective, learned and enforced policy event indices coincide. Observation acquisition,
source revision, policy version and frame epoch are recorded separately. Duplicate
delivery is idempotent; an old revision cannot replace a newer one; conflicting payloads
at one revision fail explicitly. This is a trusted synchronous fixture, not an authority
discovery, regrant, authentication or distributed ordering implementation.

## Recorded outcomes and dynamic interpretation

Each method has 960 query outcomes. Counts overlap where a false finite answer also
uses forbidden data; they are not failure probabilities.

| Method | Available | False finite answers | Needlessly unavailable | Forbidden processing | Available output from forbidden processing |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Per-query rank check | 528 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Separate permitted summaries | 528 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Require full joint rank | 360 | 0 | 168 | 0 | 0 |
| Pin gauge as absolute evidence | 960 | 432 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Keep withdrawn anchor | 768 | 240 | 0 | 480 | 384 |

All five agree numerically wherever both the candidate and reference give finite
answers. That restricted error metric alone would miss every failure in this table.
Unavailable answers are compared as statuses, not replaced by convenient zero values.

For V=1 and D=1/4, withdrawing A0 leaves d=y-x at mean 3, variance 1/4, and -2d at
mean -6, variance 1. Absolute x, y and midpoint become unavailable immediately. After
A1 arrives, external means are x=-2, y=1, midpoint=-1/2, while d is still 3. Withdrawal
of R then leaves only x, at mean -2 and variance 1/4. After the final withdrawal no
nonconstant query remains available.

Moving the coordinate origin by 1000 changes coordinate-valued means by -1000 without
changing any canonical external answer or uncertainty. Conversely, after anchor loss,
changing the gauge by 11 moves the bad control's x, y and midpoint by +11 without new
data. Its relative answers remain unchanged. A coordinate jump is therefore not itself
an estimation correction, and zero relative jump does not validate an absolute answer.

The new anchor changes the last justified external x mean from 5 to -2. The correct
methods expose the intervening unavailability and then accept -2 immediately; they do
not blend with the revoked anchor. Adjacent-available mean jumps are undefined across
that gap. Their zero canonical-jump maximum must not be read as zero reacquisition
correction or continuous consumer motion. Traces retain the gap, both endpoint values,
source acquisition events and new uncertainty. No command, plant, collision model or
physical response envelope is included. Relative-query preservation is a possible input
to a future consumer policy, not evidence that continued motion is safe.

## Cost, validation and corrections

The fixture retains at most two active entries and three revision fingerprints. The
split representation holds four active numerical payload coefficients; the raw-factor
methods hold eight (two row entries, value and variance per factor). Identity metadata,
SHA-256 conflict history, temporary matrix assembly and evaluator traces are excluded
from that coefficient count. This is not a byte measurement or general compression
benchmark. Hashes are conflict detectors here, not a privacy mechanism.

Limits are two variables, 16 entries, 32 revision fingerprints, eight deliveries per
event and 32 states. Tests cover malformed inputs, immutable input snapshots, stale or
conflicting revisions, policy bounds and history exhaustion. There is no unbounded
simulation, long-running memory claim or real-time deadline claim.

## Design implications, limits and next action

For Nestbox, distinguish external position, relative geometry and a numerical coordinate
choice. Report query validity and uncertainty at the stated frame/evidence/policy versions.
Retain useful independent components when authorized instead of reducing every result
to one global valid/invalid flag. A gauge chosen to run a solver or draw a view must
not become a measurement. Task readiness also needs uncertainty limits; a finite answer
with huge variance is not automatically usable for docking or control.

For Soradyne, preserve independent artifact identity, rights, revisions and retention
coverage without interpreting observability or covariance. Application-specific query
validity belongs above the provider boundary. Retention of an independently authorized
component can avoid unnecessary loss, but the provider cannot infer those rights from
the fact that an algebraic query cancels another input. No new shared-flow restriction
or schema change is proposed by this experiment.

The exact rank and u/v results cover one static two-variable linear likelihood with
independent noise, trusted metadata and synchronous P3. They do not establish numerical
rank thresholds, floating-point robustness, nonlinear pose/scale observability,
general summary decomposition, SE(3) covariance transport, asynchronous revocation,
crash recovery or consumer safety. Full I05/R06 and mandatory compound R06+S04 remain
open. Explicit unknown directions are not proof of an implementation's hardware safety.

Next bounded cycle: F01, with a narrow F02 replay case. Give two producers unequal
lossy deliveries before failover. Compare recovery from only a durable horizon, from
a checkpoint bound to its actual input manifest plus authorized replay, and explicit
unavailability when required retained evidence is absent. Check exact mean/uncertainty,
input identity, duplicate handling and current permissions; distinguish a published
answer from restart state. Include an authorized custodian that survives a producer
and a missing retained component. Defer crash-at-every-write, role fencing and physical
consumers to their planned later slices. This follows the existing recovery priority
after the initial I03/I04/I05 witnesses, without claiming those families complete.
